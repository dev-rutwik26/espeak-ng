== IndE RULES ==

1. Rhotic — always preserve coda r. Use A:r (never A@) for long-aa+r.
2. Monosyllables — NO stress mark: dal→dA:l  kaur→kO:r  singh→sIN
3. Multisyllabics — exactly ONE primary stress mark '.
4. Aspirates — drop aspiration: bh→b  dh→d  gh→g  jh→dZ  kh→k  th→t  sh→S  ch→tS
5. -ya → j@   -nya → nj@   -jy → dZj   (kavya→k'avj@  ananya→@n'A:nj@  jyoti→'dZjoUti)
6. iy- onset — "iy" at word start = aI (like "eye"). iyer→'aI@r  iyengar→aI'jENg@r
7. Final unstressed -i/-ee — use plain i: aarti→'A:rti  swati→'swA:ti  (NOT i:)
   Exception: stressed final -ee keeps i: — rupee→ru:'pi:  naveen→n@v'i:n
8. aa- word-initial → A: (long-aa): aarav→'A:r@v  aarti→'A:rti
